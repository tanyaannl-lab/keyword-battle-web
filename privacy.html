Privacy Notice

Operator: MCCARE COMMERCE LIMITED · Last updated October 27, 2026

This notice describes the handling of information for the Amazon Ads authorization, reporting and analysis features of MCCARE Ads Tool, an internal business tool operated by MCCARE COMMERCE LIMITED ("we", "us" or "our").

1. Scope and purpose

The tool is intended for authorized personnel working with our company's own Amazon advertising accounts. Its purpose is to prepare internal reports, analyze advertising performance and support business decisions.

This notice covers the tool's Amazon Ads features. It does not describe unrelated website features or data obtained through other integrations.

2. Information handled by the tool

Depending on the permissions granted, the APIs used and the reports requested, information handled by the tool may include:

  • Advertising account or profile identifiers, marketplace information and account details made available by Amazon.
  • Campaign, ad group, advertised product, keyword and targeting information.
  • Advertising reports and performance measures, such as impressions, clicks, spend and attributed sales or conversions.
  • Authorization codes and access or refresh tokens needed to establish and maintain an authorized API connection.
  • Information provided when contacting us about the tool, including contact details and the substance of a support or deletion request.

Amazon handles the Amazon account sign-in process. Your Amazon account password should be entered only on Amazon's sign-in page, rather than submitted to this tool.

3. How information is used

Information is used to connect authorized advertising accounts, retrieve and organize advertising information, produce internal reports and analyses, troubleshoot the integration and respond to requests. Access and use must remain within the authorization granted and applicable Amazon terms.

4. Access and disclosure

Advertising information is used for our internal business purposes, including historical analysis. We do not sell or publicly disclose advertising information.

Information may be accessed by authorized personnel and service providers needed to operate the tool, subject to applicable restrictions. Information may also be disclosed to meet a binding legal requirement or respond to a valid request from Amazon.

No external AI processing. We do not send advertising data obtained through this tool to external artificial intelligence services for analysis, summarization, model training or other processing. Reporting and historical analysis are performed within our own controlled systems.

5. Retention and deletion

Long-term historical analysis. We retain advertising reports and historical performance data on a long-term basis to compare performance across periods, assess trends and support business decisions. There is no fixed automatic 30-day or 90-day deletion period for these reports.

Long-term retention is limited to the continuing authorized business purpose and applicable Amazon requirements. It does not override a valid deletion request, Amazon-required deletion, termination requirements or other applicable restrictions. Data no longer needed for an authorized purpose is to be deleted. Authorization credentials are retained only while needed for the authorized connection.

To request deletion of information associated with an authorized account, email yiwumaicai@163.com. Identify the relevant account and information so that the request can be verified and processed. Do not include passwords, client secrets or API tokens in the request.

Stopping future API access and deleting previously retrieved information are separate actions. A deletion request should identify any stored reports or exports that also need to be addressed.

6. Protection of information

Advertising information and authorization credentials must be protected against unauthorized access, disclosure and use. Access is for authorized business purposes. Please contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access to information associated with this tool.

7. Updates

We update this notice when the tool's data handling changes. The last-updated date appears at the top of this page.
